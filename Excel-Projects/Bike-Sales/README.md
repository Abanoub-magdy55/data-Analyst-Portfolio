# 📊 Bike Sales Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

An interactive **Excel Bike Sales Dashboard** developed to analyze bike sales trends, regional performance, and top-selling bike models.

The dashboard transforms raw sales data into meaningful business insights and provides an interactive view of sales performance across different regions and bike models.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

The main objective of this project was to analyze bike sales data and provide a clear overview of sales performance across different regions and products.

The analysis focuses on:

* Monitoring bike sales trends
* Analyzing regional sales performance
* Identifying top-selling bike models
* Comparing sales across different regions
* Understanding sales patterns
* Providing an interactive reporting experience

## 🔍 Analysis Performed

### Sales Trend Analysis

Analyzed bike sales trends over time to understand changes in sales performance and identify sales patterns.

### Regional Performance

Compared bike sales across different regions to identify areas with higher and lower sales performance.

### Top-Selling Models

Analyzed bike models to identify the best-performing and top-selling products.

### Product Performance

Compared sales performance across different bike models to understand product demand and customer preferences.

### Interactive Dashboard

Created an interactive Excel dashboard using visualizations and filters to allow users to explore bike sales data from different perspectives.
