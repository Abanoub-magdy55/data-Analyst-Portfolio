# 📊 Tri-Stars Sales Performance Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

An interactive **Excel Sales Performance Dashboard** developed to analyze Tri-Stars' sales performance and profitability.

The dashboard provides a clear overview of key sales and profitability indicators, allowing users to monitor overall performance and analyze business results through interactive visualizations.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

The main objective of this project was to transform raw sales data into an interactive dashboard that provides meaningful insights into sales performance and profitability.

The analysis focuses on:

* Monitoring overall sales performance
* Analyzing sales volume
* Evaluating profitability
* Monitoring discount levels
* Measuring profit margins
* Providing an interactive reporting experience

## 📊 Key KPIs

The dashboard tracks the following key performance indicators:

* **Total Sales**
* **Total Quantity**
* **Total Profit**
* **Average Discount**
* **Profit Margin**

---

## 🔍 Analysis Performed

### Sales Performance

Analyzed total sales and sales volume to provide an overview of overall business performance.

### Quantity Analysis

Analyzed total quantity sold to understand sales volume and product movement.

### Profitability Analysis

Analyzed total profit to evaluate the financial performance generated from sales.

### Discount Analysis

Monitored average discount levels to understand the impact of discounts on sales performance.

### Profit Margin Analysis

Analyzed profit margin to evaluate profitability relative to sales.
