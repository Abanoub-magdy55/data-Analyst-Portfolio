# 📊 HR Report

## 📌 Project Overview

An interactive **Excel HR Report** developed to analyze employee productivity, experience levels, and salary distribution.

The report transforms raw HR data into meaningful insights and provides an overview of workforce characteristics, employee productivity, and salary patterns.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

The main objective of this project was to analyze employee data and provide a clear overview of workforce productivity, experience, and salary distribution.

The analysis focuses on:

* Analyzing employee productivity
* Understanding employee experience levels
* Analyzing salary distribution
* Comparing employee performance
* Identifying workforce patterns
* Providing an interactive HR reporting experience

## 🔍 Analysis Performed

### Employee Productivity

Analyzed employee productivity to understand workforce performance and identify productivity patterns.

### Experience Analysis

Analyzed employee experience levels to understand the distribution of employees based on their years of experience.

### Salary Distribution

Analyzed salary distribution to understand differences in employee compensation across different experience and workforce groups.

### Employee Performance

Compared employee productivity and performance indicators to provide insights into workforce performance.

### Workforce Analysis

Analyzed employee characteristics and workforce distribution to provide a clearer understanding of the organization.

### Interactive Report

Created an interactive Excel report using visualizations and filters to allow users to explore HR data from different perspectives.
