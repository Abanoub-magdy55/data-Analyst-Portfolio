# 📊 Customer Churn Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

An interactive **Power BI Customer Churn Analysis Dashboard** developed to analyze customer churn within a telecommunications company.

The project focuses on understanding the overall churn rate, identifying customer segments with higher churn, and analyzing the factors and patterns associated with customer attrition.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

The main objective of this project is to provide a clear view of customer churn and identify patterns that can help businesses better understand customer attrition.

The analysis focuses on:

* Measuring overall customer churn
* Identifying customer segments with higher churn rates
* Analyzing customer behavior
* Understanding churn patterns
* Comparing churn across different customer characteristics
* Providing interactive insights through data visualization

## 📊 Key KPIs

The dashboard provides insights into key customer churn indicators, including:

* Total Customers
* Churned Customers
* Churn Rate
* Customer Retention
* Customer Segmentation
* Churn by Customer Characteristics

---

## 🔍 Analysis Performed

### Customer Churn Analysis

Analyzed the overall customer churn rate and compared churned customers with retained customers.

### Customer Segmentation

Analyzed churn across different customer segments to identify groups with higher levels of customer attrition.

### Churn Patterns

Explored customer characteristics and behavioral patterns associated with churn.

### Interactive Analysis

Created interactive visuals and filters allowing users to analyze churn from different perspectives.

### DAX Analysis

Developed DAX measures and calculated columns to support KPI calculations and analytical insights.
